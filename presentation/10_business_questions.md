# 10 Business Questions Solved - Indus Weave Textiles

### Q1: Month-end headcount by plant and department
**What it is:** The exact number of employees in each plant and department at the end of every month.
**CFO Presentation:** "Sir, we analyzed headcount for 6 months. We have the exact data for every plant and department to see where staff is increasing or decreasing."
*(File: q01_headcount.csv)*

### Q2: Monthly attrition rate by department
**What it is:** The rate at which employees are leaving the company (turnover rate).
**CFO Presentation:** "Departments with high attrition rates (like Sales or Weaving) need a focus on employee retention, otherwise, the cost of hiring and training new staff will increase."
*(File: q02_attrition.csv)*

### Q3: Absenteeism rate by plant and month
**What it is:** Employees being absent from work without approved leave.
**CFO Presentation:** "When people are absent without approval, it impacts production. We identified which plant has the highest absenteeism."
*(File: q03_absenteeism.csv)*

### Q4: Top 10 employees with most late arrivals
**What it is:** The top 10 employees who arrive late most frequently.
**CFO Presentation:** "These employees are consistently late. It reduces productivity at the start of the shift. HR needs to counsel them."
*(File: q04_late_arrivals.csv)*

### Q5: Overtime: Computed vs Approved vs Paid
**What it is:** Actual overtime worked (from biometrics) vs. approved vs. paid.
**CFO Presentation:** "This is the main issue, sir! The system approves one thing, and biometrics show another. For example, HR worked 10 hours but was paid for 405 hours. This is a major leakage."
*(File: q05_overtime_reconciliation.csv)*

### Q6: Departments where paid overtime exceeds computed by >15%
**What it is:** Departments where overtime pay is 15% higher than actual work done.
**CFO Presentation:** "These are the specific departments where more than 15% of money is being wasted. They need an immediate audit."
*(File: q06_excess_overtime.csv)*

### Q7: Employees paid with zero attendance (Ghost Employees)
**What it is:** Employees who received salary but had zero attendance.
**CFO Presentation:** "These are ghost employees. They are getting paid without working. They should be blocked immediately."
*(File: q07_ghost_employees.csv)*

### Q8: Employees still receiving pay after separation date
**What it is:** People who left the job but are still getting paid.
**CFO Presentation:** "Some people have left the company, but the payroll system is still paying them. The Payroll and HR systems need to be linked."
*(File: q08_pay_after_separation.csv)*

### Q9: Payroll cost per employee by department (MoM change)
**What it is:** Month-over-month salary cost for each department.
**CFO Presentation:** "This is our overall expenditure. We track if the cost decreased when the headcount decreased."
*(File: q09_payroll_cost_mom.csv)*

### Q10: Employees whose approved Annual leave > full-year entitlement
**What it is:** Employees who took more annual leave than their yearly allowance.
**CFO Presentation:** "These employees have taken more leave than their annual entitlement, which is against policy. Their extra leave should be marked as leave without pay."
