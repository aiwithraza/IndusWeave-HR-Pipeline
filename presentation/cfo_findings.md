# CFO Findings & Action Plan

## Finding 1: Overtime Leakage
**Evidence:** In departments like Human Resources and Production Planning, the overtime paid is significantly higher (>15%) than what is computed from biometric punches. For example, in HR, employees actually worked only 10 hours, but payroll paid for 405 hours.
**Action:** Integrate Payroll directly with biometric computed overtime rather than manual approved requests.

## Finding 2: Faulty Biometric Device
**Evidence:** Device `DEV_05` had a clock running 1 hour ahead. Found by comparing average check-in times against shift start times.
**Action:** Send IT team to calibrate the clock, and implement auto-sync for all devices.

## Finding 3: Ghost Employees
**Evidence:** Employees in Weaving and Cutting departments received payroll earnings in Sept 2026 without a single recorded attendance punch.
**Action:** Freeze payroll for zero-attendance cases until physical verification is done.
